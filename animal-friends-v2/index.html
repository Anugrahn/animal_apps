    How the lessons work

    The four core subjects are modelled on the curriculum Danbi Education describes for its Wink home-learning programme (Korea; Animal Friends is not affiliated with it): short daily lessons, each one a small road from a new idea to using it: concept → practice → problem-solving → review.

    • Phonics (4 steps): letter sounds a–z, short and long vowels, blends and digraphs, silent letters and tricky sounds. Each lesson: Listen → Trace → Play → Quiz.
    • English (4 stages): basic expressions, everyday expressions, my world, reading and grammar. Each lesson: Story → Words → Say it → Quiz.
    • Math (5 levels): every level covers the six areas — numbers, calculation, shapes, measuring, patterns and data — from first counting (3–4 years) to the upper primary years. Each lesson: Story → Real life → Play → Quiz.
    • Bahasa Indonesia (4 tahap): huruf a–z, suku kata, suku kata lanjutan, membaca kalimat. Each lesson: Kenal → Tebalkan → Kuasai.

    Today’s Study plans 10–15 minutes: two core subjects on each weekday, in turn (Phonics and Math, then English and Bahasa), plus one Fun & Joy game. A lesson not finished comes back the next day. At the weekend there is nothing new: two lessons already played come back for review. Every stage ends with a review lesson before its treasure chest.

    Ages: the first levels suit children from about 3; later levels reach early primary school. Use Open every level below to let an older child start further on.
