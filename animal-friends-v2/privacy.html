Privacy

Naura’s Wonderf4ur World is a learning app for young children. Last updated 13 October 2026.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We collect nothing. Your child's progress stays on this device.
  • The microphone is only used if a grown-up turns it on.

What is stored, and where

Your child's friend, stars, settings and play time are saved in this browser on this device (its local storage), so the app can remember them. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the browser's data, or "Start over" in the grown-up area, removes them.

The app keeps a copy of itself on the device so it works without the internet.

Speaking practice (Say It!)

A grown-up chooses how it works, behind a grown-up check:

  • No microphone: nothing is recorded.
  • Echo: records a few seconds and plays it straight back. The recording stays on the device and is thrown away.
  • Voice check: uses the browser's own speech recognition, which sends the audio to the browser's maker to be recognised (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge), under their privacy policies. It is off unless a grown-up turns it on.

The voice you hear

Most lines are recordings that come with the app. A line with no recording (such as a name your child typed) is read by a voice built into the device. The app picks a voice on the device, so the words aren't sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side. The app makes no requests to other companies' servers.
